Interpretation

9B→4B

The teacher is close in raw velocity magnitude but systematically anti-aligned with x0 detail, explaining gradual blur despite tighter matching.

HY→4B

HY reproduces the same anti-detail signature and is geometrically closer to 9B than to the successful 32B teacher.

32B→4B

A much larger correction is beneficial because it reinforces spatial gradients and raises one-step x0 total variation.
