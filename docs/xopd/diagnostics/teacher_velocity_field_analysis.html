Interpretation

9B and HY
Both are relatively close to 4B, but their correction directions remove spatial derivative energy. HY is more aligned with 9B than with 32B and has even stronger latent anti-detail cosine.
32B
The correction is 4–5× larger, yet adds gradient/Laplacian detail and mid-trajectory high-frequency power. A magnitude-only trust region cannot separate these cases.

Recommended selective-OPD metric

c = cos(grad(x0_student), grad(x0_teacher − x0_student))
Use a detached sample-step mask or soft gate. Keep samples in every microbatch and zero only their per-sample loss, preserving gradient accumulation and collective ordering. Always compare with a random mask at the same keep rate.
